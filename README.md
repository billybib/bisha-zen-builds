# Bisha London Elevate

Redesign the existing Bisha Contractors website (bishacontractors.com) into a premium, modern London construction/renovation website.
IMPORTANT: Do not simply reskin the existing website. Re-think the information hierarchy and simplify the experience.
CORE IDEA: The current site tries to communicate too many services and too much information at once. The redesign should feel calm, confident and spacious.
POSITIONING

Bisha, est. 2010
Premium, calm, confident,
Higher-end than it currently is, but keeping the personal touch, class but still approachable, Bisha majorly Levelled up, keep the logo in...


Think:
•	Premium London residential construction
•	Large photography
•	Generous white space
•	Strong typography
•	Minimal navigation
•	Very clear calls to action
•	Let the quality of the work speak for itself
Avoid:
•	Clutter
•	Huge lists of services
•	Walls of text
•	Generic construction imagery
•	Excessive animations
•	Cheap/template-looking UI
•	Overly corporate language
•	Making every service equally prominent
HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE:
1.	HERO Use a large, high-quality project image.
Headline: "Building Better Homes Across London." 
Supporting text: "Extensions, renovations and construction projects, delivered with care from first conversation to final finish."
Primary CTA: "Start your project"
Secondary CTA: "View our work"
***
2.	SELECTED PROJECTS Make completed projects the visual centrepiece.
Show 3–4 large project cards with:
•	Project image
•	Project type
•	Location
•	Short one-line description
Example: "North London Extension" "Rear extension · Full renovation"
Each should lead to a detailed project page.
3.	WHAT WE DO Do NOT list every individual service.
Group services into 4–5 broad categories:
Extensions – Renovations- Loft Conversions -Kitchens & Bathrooms -General Construction
Each category should have a short, human description and link to further information.
4.	WHY BISHA — short intro + 5 points: Quality workmanship,
   Experience, Communication, Reliability, Attention to detail.
   No marketing clichés.
Focus on:
•	Quality workmanship
•	Experience
•	Communication
•	Reliability
•	Attention to detail
Do not use generic marketing clichés.
5.	TESTIMONIALS Use genuine existing customer testimonials where available.
Make the reviews visually prominent but elegant.
6.	ABOUT Introduce Bisha Contractors as a real local construction company.
Keep this human and concise.
Avoid generic "We are a leading construction company..." copy.
7.	FINAL CTA Large, simple closing section:
"Planning your next project?"
"Tell us what you're thinking about and let's see how we can help."
CTA: "Discuss your project"
Include phone/contact options.
DESIGN DIRECTION:
Use a restrained, premium palette. Primarily whites, warm neutrals, charcoal/dark text and natural photography.
Large typography. Generous margins. Strong visual hierarchy. Subtle hover effects and transitions only where they improve usability.
The website should feel closer to high-end than it currently is, although keeping the personal touch apparent.
MOBILE: Design mobile-first and make the project photography and CTA experience excellent on mobile.
MOST IMPORTANT: The website must feel LESS busy than the current website.
Do not try to fit everything above the fold. Do not make every service, statistic or selling point compete for attention.
The desired emotional response is:
"This company looks established, capable and trustworthy. Their work looks excellent. I want to speak to them."
Build the homepage first to a highly polished standard before expanding secondary pages.
